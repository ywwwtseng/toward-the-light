About

The Story

One person, one suitcase, and a city that slowly learns his name. Through lit corridors, an all-night store, a bus stop in the rain and a small rented room, twenty-four scenes follow the days he keeps walking forward.

He rows through the rain, finds his way through the fog, and shapes the scattered pieces into something new. Each night leaves him a little brighter. In the end he understands: the light was never waiting at the finish line. It was in every step.

Walk again

The Concept

At the end of the road, a ring of light keeps one place open. It is room for the next light, for whoever is still on the way.

Scrolling is time. Scroll down and the days move forward; scroll up to look back on the road already walked. The city turns from daytime fog to night-time lights and back to morning, and he walks through all of it, toward the light.

For everyone building a life in a new city: you are already shining, and the light is on its way to you too.
