About

The Story

One person, one suitcase, and a city slowly getting to know him. From lit corridors and all-night convenience stores to a bus stop in the rain and a small rented room, twenty-four scenes trace the days he keeps moving forward.

He has rowed through the rain, found his way through the fog, and gathered scattered pieces one by one. Every night taught him something. In the end he understood: the light is not at the finish line. It is in every step.

The Concept

The circle keeps one space open. It is not something missing; it is room for the next light. A note to everyone making their way alone in the city: you are shining, and the light is waiting for you too.

Scrolling is time. Scroll down and the days move forward; scroll up to look back on the road behind. The city drifts between daytime fog and night-time lights, walking with him all the way toward the light.
