## What changed in `makeHero()` (audit §99, items 1–6)

1. **Shoulders (no plateau).** The torso loft is widest at the chest (1.30, .52 wide) and tapers continuously to the collar base (1.38 .48 → 1.45 .36 → 1.51 .26 → 1.56 .18) instead of the old .54/.56 shelf at 1.3–1.4 that collapsed to .18 at 1.52. Shoulder pivots moved in to x ±.23 (height kept at 1.36 so the desk/boat hand placement is unchanged). The sleeve gets a small cap ring above the pivot, offset 2.5 cm inward, so the shoulder rounds into the slope with no step. Visible in skyline 5.65 (back view) and office 3.x (walk from behind).
2. **Collar hides the neck.** A dark collar ring (coat colour × .55, 6-sided, 1.47 → 1.605, a little wider than the head) rises out of the shoulder slope, with a turtleneck in the inner tone inside it up to the jaw (1.635). The neck loft is in the inner tone. From behind (skyline, office) the pale neck stick is gone; from the front the chin sits on the collar. The same dark tone makes the sleeve cuffs (the palm still starts inside the cuff). The coat's open front (the V) is now a bent strip that follows the chest bulge, tucked under the collar, instead of a flat triangle.
3. **Coat skirt.** Standing/walking: the two panels share one jitter seed and overlap at the centre line, so their back faces shade as one plane; the split line down the back is gone (gait sheet, office). Past −1 rad on the thigh (sit, curl) each panel hides and two pieces take over: a lap piece hugging the thigh at 100 % follow, and a flap hinged on the knee group that hangs down the shin front to mid-shin. The fixed seat piece swaps to a short version ending at .9 so nothing hangs under a chair seat: the "side table" block under the desk chair (desk 4.x), in the boat (flood), on the stool (store) and on the bench (call) is gone, and the shards curl reads as knees-to-chest with the coat lying over the thighs. Lying (room): legs straight, so the standing panels show; the coat is still a rigid slab (inherent to a rigid skirt) but no longer a box under the thighs.
4. **Legs and tone.** Trouser default 0x1a → 0x2a; the shoe colour is derived (trousers × .5, capped at 0x10) so the legs are always one tone lighter than the shoes, also for the scene overrides (office 0x3a, flood 0x2a, shards 0x0e). Thighs .14/.15 → .15/.16 with a gusset ring inside the pelvis, shins .09→.06 → .105→.09 with a knee cap ring and a turned-up cuff at the hem. Legs now read in the dark scenes (office, desk, call) instead of vanishing as sticks.
5. **Shoes.** Six-sided plan (flat heel, flat toe, widest at the ball), 25 cm long, 9 cm tall at the heel, the instep sloping down from inside the trouser cuff, a sole lip at −.04/−.06. Lateral spread of the default stance widened a little (legs z −.035 / +.03) so the shoes keep a 12 cm gap at mid-stride.
6. **Fringe.** One bent plate (two facets) lying on the hair/forehead surface, corners on the hair shell, the tip 1 cm proud of the forehead, with a darker vertex tone, instead of the four-facet blade whose apex stood 2.3 cm in front of the face. It no longer rises above the head when the head tilts back (petals 14.5).

Triangles: hero 1093 → 1525 (+40 %, the hands alone are 544 of both); all lofts are 4–6 sides, flat shaded, no new materials beyond one derived grey per figure.

## Concerns / things I could not resolve

- **Scene 2 at mid-stride**: the camera looks almost along the walking line, so the two shoes overlap *in projection* whenever the feet pass each other (they also did before: compare `before_crack_closeup.png`). In 3D they never touch: the feet are .22 apart centre to centre and the shoes .10 wide, a 12 cm gap. A wider stance would look bow-legged, so I left it.
- **Lying pose** is still a straight slab because the skirt pieces are rigid; a bend would need a spine, which is out of scope.
- **Petals 14.5** (head back −.55): the bright wedge on top of the head is the lit underside of the gem head (skin) seen from below, bordered by the hair rim; the old fringe horn above the silhouette is gone, but the face itself still reads bright from that angle.
- The seat/lap/flap switch is a hard threshold (−1 rad on the thigh); no scene animates across it, but one that did would pop. Documented in CHARACTER.md.
